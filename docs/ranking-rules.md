# Farflung leaderboard rules, summarised

From the [leaderboard rules](https://farflunggame.fun/leaderboards/rules) of [Farflung](https://farflunggame.fun/).

## Daily clock

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| New challenge | 00:00 UTC |
| Max expedition length | 10 minutes from start |
| Previous board closes | By 00:10 UTC; full answers can then go to the archive |

## Ties

Equal scores share a rank. If two players are first, the next score is third (1, 1, 3). Neither speed nor display order changes rank.

With fewer than 30 results, the board shows actual positions and participant counts and makes no percentile claims.

## Weekly score

- The UTC week runs Monday to Sunday.
- Your best five valid daily results are added; missing days count as zero.
- A normal week's maximum is 2,500 km (5 × 500).
- The board shows which dates count.

Private leagues use the same official results and rules.

## Corrections

If a question is wrong, a moderator can void that round consistently across affected attempts. The board and the maximum possible score are then recomputed.

Maintained by the developer of farflunggame.fun, an independent edition.
