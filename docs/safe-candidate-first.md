# Save a safe candidate before hunting rare answers

From the guide [How to find unexpected countries](https://farflunggame.fun/guides/find-unexpected-countries) on [Farflung](https://farflunggame.fun/).

1. **Start with one you know.** Select a safe candidate early and wait for the saved confirmation. A valid saved selection is your fallback if time runs out; you have not committed until you pin.
2. **Read the qualification.** A border clue is incomplete without its scope: land borders only? Do territories count? Spend a few seconds on the scope line.
3. **Then look for the rare one.** Swap in an unusual country only once you are sure it qualifies.
