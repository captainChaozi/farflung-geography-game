# Study topics and clue reading

Notes for [Farflung](https://farflunggame.fun/), a free daily geography puzzle: five clues, pin a country that fits each, and less obvious correct answers score more.

## Reading a clue
1. **Relation** – borders, lies in, has a capital, crosses a line.
2. **Limit** – land only, Europe only, a displayed name, a stated dataset.
3. **Unit** – country, island and territory are different things.

Worked example: for "a country with a land border with France in Europe", Belgium, Monaco and Andorra qualify; Brazil fails the European limit; the United Kingdom fails the land-border condition.
