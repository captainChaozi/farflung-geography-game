# Farflung 6 Nov: the nine land neighbours of DR Congo

Source: [Farflung Daily archive, 6 Nov 2026](https://farflunggame.fun/archive/2026-11-06) · Game: [Farflung](https://farflunggame.fun/) · More drills: [geography topics](https://farflunggame.fun/topics)

Field Note 03 asked for a country that shares a land border with the **Democratic Republic of the Congo**. The game uses its pinned atlas land-neighbour graph, limited to the 195 playable entries. Overseas territories, maritime boundaries and bridges do not add neighbours.

## The nine answers

| Country | Tier | km |
|---|---|---|
| Angola | Rare | 70 |
| Tanzania | Rare | 70 |
| Uganda | Rare | 70 |
| Rwanda | Elusive | 85 |
| Zambia | Elusive | 85 |
| Burundi | Farflung | 100 |
| Central African Republic | Farflung | 100 |
| Republic of the Congo | Farflung | 100 |
| South Sudan | Farflung | 100 |

## Memory aid

Walk clockwise from the north: Central African Republic, South Sudan, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Tanzania (across Lake Tanganyika), Zambia, Angola, then Republic of the Congo to the west.

Angola counts once, even though its Cabinda exclave also touches DR Congo.
