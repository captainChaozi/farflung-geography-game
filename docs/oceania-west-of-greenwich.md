## The clue

*Find a country in Oceania whose atlas label lies west of Greenwich.*

"West of Greenwich" means a negative longitude, between 0° and 180°W. Most of Oceania sits east of Greenwich, so the answer set is tiny.

## Valid answers

| Country | Score |
|---|---|
| Kiribati | 100 km |
| Samoa | 85 km |
| Tonga | 100 km |

## Why these three

The clue uses each country's atlas label point (Natural Earth 5.1.2). Samoa and Tonga sit just east of the 180° line, at roughly 172–175°W, so their labels fall in the western hemisphere. Kiribati spans both sides of 180°; its label point puts it west of Greenwich in this atlas.
