# What the independent Farflung edition is, and what it is not

Source: [About this independent Farflung edition](https://farflunggame.fun/about) · Play: [Farflung daily geography game](https://farflunggame.fun/)

People sometimes arrive at farflunggame.fun expecting the original Farflung. This note sums up what the About page says so the difference is clear before you play.

## It is a separate build

- Independently developed and operated, **not affiliated with or endorsed by** the creators of the original game. The original lives at farflung.game.
- The question bank, scoring pipeline, code, illustrations and sound design were made for this edition.

## What the game looks like

- A shared **five-round daily** challenge. Each correct pin earns distance, and rarer correct answers earn more, up to 500 km per expedition. Kilometres are game points.
- **Unlimited** practice with a timer, and untimed **Wander** for learning the map.
- A public [archive of closed expeditions](https://farflunggame.fun/archive/2026-11-11) with answers, sources and anonymous choice tables.

## What it deliberately leaves out

- No paid attempts, score boosts or random purchases.
- Discovery stamps record what you learned; they never improve a competitive score.
- Sound is optional, and animations respect reduced-motion settings.

## How it explains itself

Question references appear with results, the scoring page explains estimates and historical data, and the country rules explain scope (territories, enclaves, bridges). Reported mistakes go through a visible correction process.
